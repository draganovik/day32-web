Day32 privacy information

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Day32 is a client-side calendar manager. It connects directly to Google Calendar from your browser and does not operate its own user-account or calendar database.

Google account access

When you connect Google, Google handles sign-in and asks you to approve the permissions shown on its consent screen. Day32 requests basic profile information, read access to your calendar list, and access to manage calendar events. It uses this access only to display calendars and to create, update, or delete events when you request those actions.

Storage and tokens

  • Google access tokens are kept in browser memory and are not written to local storage.
  • Calendar and event data are not persisted by Day32.
  • Your theme preference may be stored locally in your browser.
  • The installed app shell may be cached by the service worker for offline startup.

Reloading or closing the app clears its in-memory connection. Disconnecting also asks Google to revoke the current access token.

Third-party services

Google processes authentication and Calendar API requests under its own terms and privacy policies. The hosting provider may process ordinary web-server information such as IP address, user agent, and request metadata. Day32 does not include advertising or analytics trackers in the application.

Your choices

You can stop using the connection at any time by selecting Disconnect in Day32. You can also review or remove Day32 access from your Google Account permissions.
